Google SERP overlay examples

Reference crops for “search Google → stay on the results page → screenshot + crop” overlays. We do not click into destination sites. Router picks a tab from the spoken cue; crop is tight to the useful card/photo only.

Images

Event / atmosphere photo

Spoken cue: “We were at that Asbury festival last summer…”

Query: Asbury Park music festival stage night

Crop: one Images tile + tiny source line — not the full Images grid.

Flights

Itinerary card

Spoken cue: “I flew Newark to Miami that Friday for about two hundred bucks…”

Query: flights EWR to MIA Friday

Crop: single Flights result card (route, time, price) — not the whole Flights chrome.

Web · AI Overview

Summarized answer box

Spoken cue: “They’re reopening the boardwalk for weekend events…”

Query: New Jersey boardwalk summer weekend events reopen

Crop: AI Overview / featured summary card only, including citation chips.

Web / News

Headline + snippet

Spoken cue: “The city just approved that waterfront park expansion…”

Query: Jersey City waterfront park expansion approved

Crop: top organic result block (title, URL, snippet) — no ads row.

Maps / Places

Place card

Spoken cue: “We met at that little Italian place in Hoboken…”

Query: Italian restaurant Hoboken

Crop: place panel (photo, rating, hours) — not the full map canvas.
