# Shelves (what exists — not what we’ve wired)

Official catalogs only. “Top 10 per category” doesn’t fit Remotion: most
official groups have 3–6 items. Take **the whole official Element set** as
the Remotion shelf, and **ten video-usable looks per Bits group** as the
Bits shelf. Star in Studio / on the sites; we wire what you star.

## Remotion official Elements

Source: https://www.remotion.dev/elements  
In Studio: Actions → Browse Elements → Remotion Elements.

There is no official “math” pack (that’s Manim). Charts & data is the
closest. Maps are Remotion-only; Bits has none.

### Charts & data (all 5)
Horizontal bar, vertical bar, line, pie, number counter.

### Maps (all 3)
A-to-B flyover, spinning globe, watercolor map.

### Text effects (all 6)
Circle marker, text marker, strike-through, crossed-off, cylinder text, spinning text wheel.

### Captions (all 5)
Basic, rounded, moving pill, popping word, word highlight.

### Overlays (all 3)
Name lower third, location lower third, social safe zones.

### YouTube (all 3)
Comment highlight, end card, subscribe nudge.

### Backgrounds (all 6)
Notebook paper, paper texture, rotating starburst, moving waves, moving zigzags, liquid contours.

### Layouts (all 3)
Rotating cards, picture-in-picture transition, slide to split screen.

### Storytelling (all 4)
News article highlight, speed lines, on-screen messages, polaroid pictures.

### Effects (all 4)
Wiggling callout, peel, shine, tear apart.

### Audio (all 3)
Oscilloscope, voice note, mirrored spectrum.

**First ten to click in Studio if you want a talking-head starter:**
line chart, vertical bar, pie, number counter, A-to-B map, globe, name lower third, word-highlight captions, popping-word captions, YouTube comment highlight.

## React Bits (finite site)

Source: https://reactbits.dev — not video-native. Looks only; we add motion
with Remotion’s clock. No maps, no charts.

Popularity isn’t published. These are the ten per group that actually read
on a talking-head overlay (no “follow the mouse” required).

### Text (10)
Split Text, Blur Text, Count Up, Text Type, Gradient Text, Glitch Text, Shuffle, Decrypted Text, Rotating Text, Shiny Text.

### Cards / UI (10)
Profile Card, Spotlight Card, Glass Surface, Tilted Card, Card Swap, Stack, Circular Carousel, Bounce Cards, Holo Card, Counter.

### Backgrounds (10)
Aurora, Silk, Beams, Particles, Lightning, Liquid Chrome, Galaxy, Soft Aurora, Light Rays, Plasma.

Skip for video until we fake a cursor: Text Cursor, Splash Cursor, Magnet, scroll-reveal, anything “tilts toward the pointer.”
